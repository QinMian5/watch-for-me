# Reflective media

Follow the experience the speaker returns to. Describe what happened and how they understood it at the time. When their understanding changes, keep the particular observation, feedback, or consequence they say made them reconsider, alongside the interpretation it led them to.

Stay with the speaker's first-person perspective. Keep competing explanations and doubts in their own terms, especially when they are guessing what someone else felt or meant. The reflection can end with the question the speaker still has.

Read the Notes alone and identify what changed the speaker's mind and what remains uncertain. Follow the citations to the initial judgment, the turning point, and the later interpretation wherever the speaker gives them. Check each future intention against its cited words. “Maybe we'll talk again” leaves another conversation open. A commitment needs the speaker's express agreement to act.
