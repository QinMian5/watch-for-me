# Reflective media

Organize the Notes around the experience-to-understanding chain:

```text
observations and experiences
→ the speaker's interpretation
→ tensions or alternatives
→ emerging understanding
→ stated intentions
```

- Keep the first-person perspective and its shifts in certainty.
- Distinguish what the speaker experienced from explanations proposed afterward.
- Build themes from the source's own examples.
- Keep tentative intentions tentative and open questions open, so the reflection stays the speaker's own. Include a commitment only when the speaker makes one.
