# Procedural material

Organize the Notes around the goal-to-result chain:

```text
goal and prerequisites
→ ordered actions
→ visible state changes
→ checks and corrections
→ final result
```

- Establish where the reader starts, what must already be in place, and which actions depend on earlier ones. Keep commands, settings, warnings, and confirmation steps that affect the result.
- At a fork, put each condition beside its action and explain why it changes the next step. Identify optional refinements where the source offers them.
- At a check, name the observable sign that lets the reader continue or calls for a correction. If the demonstration goes wrong, trace the symptom, what was changed, and the state reached on retry. If the source predicts a result or skips the check, make that boundary clear.
- Using the Notes alone, choose the next action at a consequential step and say how to judge its outcome. Follow its citations to verify the starting state, action, and result. When the judgment depends on the screen, inspect and cite the fewest frames that show the decisive change.
