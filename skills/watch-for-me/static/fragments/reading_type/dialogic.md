# Dialogic media

Organize the Notes around the question-to-position chain:

```text
question or tension
→ participant positions
→ supporting reasons and examples
→ agreement, disagreement, or refinement
→ remaining question
```

- Attribute each paragraph's claims and judgments with `speakers`, and split different participants' views into separate paragraphs.
- Distinguish shared conclusions from unresolved disagreement.
- Explain the reasons, assumptions, and examples behind each consequential position.
- Synthesize the understanding produced by the exchange after presenting its distinct voices.
- Use displayed evidence or materials when they clarify the exchange.
- Distinguish reports, questions, feedback, recommendations, decisions, and commitments. Explain the reasons behind feedback, attributing each opinion to its speaker.
- Extract action items only when supported: write the task and stated deadline in the text, put a speaker who owns it in `speakers`, and record the source blocks in `cites`. Say when an owner or deadline is not specified, and keep suggestions separate from accepted commitments.
- Preserve unequal speaking time and substantive interruptions. Use `speakers` for anonymous speakers too, because the page shows their labels, and let the Notes follow the conversation's actual shape.
