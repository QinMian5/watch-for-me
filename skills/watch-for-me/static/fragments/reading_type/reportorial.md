# Reportorial material

Organize the Notes around the event-to-understanding chain:

```text
event or subject
→ timeline and contributing sources
→ evidence presented
→ interpretation and uncertainty
→ current conclusion
```

- Preserve chronology where sequence affects meaning.
- Attribute evidence, testimony, and interpretation to their stated sources.
- Separate observed events from narration or inference.
- Explain how the evidence changes the reader's understanding of the event or subject.
- Connect chronology, contributing causes, evidence, and uncertainty into a coherent account.
- Use maps, data, documents, and visible event evidence when they support the central account.
