# Media Page acceptance list

Repository tests compare golden files and exercise hostile text, but they do not drive a browser. After a change to `skills/media-shared/media-render/assets/`, render `tests/media-render/fixtures/` as a bundle and check these items by hand in a real browser at a `file://` URL. Report what was covered and which browsers and devices were not.

1. The Notes show by default: side by side when wide, tabs when narrow. The title is the source's own `media.title`, untranslated, in the tab and the header.
2. When wide, scrolling either pane moves the other. In mid-scroll, the current paragraph changes when its start reaches 40% of the pane height, where both starts align. The first paragraph is highlighted on opening. Every paragraph except those citing earlier material gets a turn. Both panes reach their ends together, and the current paragraph and blocks have a tinted background. Beside the Notes or on the Summary tab, the Contents repeat the Notes outline line for line: the orientation, each section with its time range and its units indented under it with the time of their earliest cited block, then the closing units without a time. The Notes show the same section headings and unit headings. On the Transcript tab, the Contents list only its sections. After a Contents jump, that heading is current and, when wide, the other pane follows, with a section's heading level with the same section in the Transcript.
3. Sync stays aligned after switching language, filtering speakers, and resizing the window.
4. When wide, a time chip or back-reference scrolls only the other pane, puts the target beside the clicked paragraph or block, and highlights it. A small scroll in either pane afterwards moves the other smoothly instead of snapping, and the usual alignment returns within one pane height of scrolling from the jump (longer for a very distant target), or by the nearer end when there is room, so both panes then reach their ends together. Only a jump whose offset is too large for the room left keeps part of it at the end, rather than snapping. When narrow, it switches to the Transcript and highlights, and both "Back to Summary" and the browser's Back button return to the original position. Opening the page at `#S012` when narrow shows the Transcript with its own Contents. With a search active, the count and Enter follow the pane now showing, after a jump and after going back.
5. Consecutive citations across sections show two chips (S012 and S013 in "Source boundary").
6. A back-reference badge returns to the Summary. A block cited several times (S012) lists unit headings.
7. Both / Original / Translation affects only the Transcript. Side-by-side text stacks at 640px or less. When every `translation` is null, there is no switch.
8. Searching `shadow` when wide finds 6 in Both (1 Summary, 5 Transcript), and 2 in Translation with a note that 4 more are in the other language. Enter and ↑↓ step through hits. Searching `幂等键` finds 6. In text containing `İ`, hits after it highlight exactly the searched word.
9. The speaker filter is multi-select, applies to both panes, and leaves "N hidden" dividers. Summary paragraphs without `speakers` stay. Following a citation or back-reference to something the filter hid clears the filter and shows the target.
10. Labels display as specified: names, "Speaker 1", grey italic "Unidentified", and `Dana Lee?`. Overlapping speech shows no tag or extra indent, and same-speaker blocks stay in one turn. No job titles appear anywhere. Each block's time is on its own line at the block's start.
11. The source line beside the title names each language in itself, such as `English → 简体中文`, shows no limitation count, and opens source details only; a timed limitation appears only under the first block that starts inside its range, and whole-source limitations are not shown. No term underlines, term tips, or term tables appear. The recording-gap stripe sits between S012 and S013.
12. Frames appear only in the Transcript when wide and also in the Summary when narrow. Thumbnails keep the image's own aspect ratio without cropping and carry no time or ID caption. They open large on click. Deleting an image file shows placeholder text.
13. Dark mode follows the system. A non-Chinese `target_language` gives an English interface.
14. Text containing `</script>` or `<img onerror>` displays literally and runs nothing.
15. There is no horizontal scrolling at 390px.
16. A bundle of 2000 or more blocks scrolls and searches without noticeable lag.
17. Renaming: the pencil enters editing without filtering, and the name only filters. Clicking another pencil while editing commits the current name and starts the next. Esc does not save. With no changes there is no copy button. Several renames share one button, its prompt holds them all, and reverting a name removes it. Labels preview across the page, and discarding restores them. After an agent applies the prompt, the page and `source_map.md` show the new names.
18. When narrow, the Notes and Transcript tabs open at the counterpart of what sits at the top: a paragraph's first cited block, the paragraph whose cites span a block, or the same section, at the same height. From the top of the page they open at the top. Switching straight back without scrolling returns to the exact place, and tapping the active tab does nothing.
19. The header is the same at every width and stays at the top: title, source summary, Contents and search; speakers; view and language. When narrow it wraps into rows, and the source summary shows only the duration.
